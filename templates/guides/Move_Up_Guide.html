Your Move Up Calculator Guide

How to see what your current home puts toward your next one, and what the payment looks like.

1

Sell, Then Buy

Two columns, the answers on top

Sell: enter what you expect your current home to sell for and what you still owe. The calculator shows your walk out the door proceeds.

Buy: enter the price of your next home. Your proceeds become the down payment, after lender, title and closing costs. Add any extra cash in Additional down payment.

The three monthly payments use a 2-1 buydown: two points lower in Year 1, one point lower in Year 2, then the full rate for Years 3–30. Each includes taxes, insurance and HOA.

2

The Math

Tap the arrow to see every number

Under each column, the Math arrow opens the full calculation: commission, Grantor’s Tax and fees on the sale; lender fees, title, prepaids and the buydown on the purchase; the loan amount, taxes and insurance.

The note rate and who pays the buydown can be changed there. A warning appears if the down payment falls under 20%, since mortgage insurance is not included.

Estimates for planning only. Your lender’s Loan Estimate is the final word.

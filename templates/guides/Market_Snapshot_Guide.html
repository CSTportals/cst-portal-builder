Your Market Snapshot Guide

What each part of the weekly CST Market Snapshot tells you, and how to read it.

1

Who Controls the Market

The headline answers it in one line

Every Market Snapshot opens with the answer: buyers, sellers or neutral. The flag under it tells you how strongly.

The line beneath gives the two numbers behind the call: how many homes listed in the last 30 days went under contract, and how many went under contract in the last two weeks.

Updated every week, so you see the market your home is selling into today, not last spring.

2

How Homes Are Selling

Success rate, days on market and price to list

Success Rate is the share of homes that sold rather than coming off the market unsold.

Days on Market and Sale Price to List Price show how fast homes sold and how close to their asking price. The gauges run red to green, so you can read them at a glance.

Below them are the most recent sales and withdrawals, colored the same way.

3

Where the Market Is Heading

Momentum: what is happening right now

Momentum looks at the homes still on the market: how many are under contract, how many of the last 30 days’ listings already have a buyer, and how long the rest have waited.

This is the reading that tells us whether to price conservatively, at the first threshold under your home’s value, or to test above it.

4

The Year So Far

Inventory, sales and success rate against last year

Year-to-date inventory and sales compared with last year, and the share of this year’s listings that have sold.

The bar underneath places the market on a single scale from Buyers to Sellers.

5

Market Conditions Change. Winning Strategies Don’t.™

The Casey Samson Team against the rest of the market

Every Market Snapshot ends with the CST track record side by side with the rest of the market: success rate, days on market, price to list, and the share sold in the first 10 days and above list.

Whatever the market is doing, the strategy is what moves your result.

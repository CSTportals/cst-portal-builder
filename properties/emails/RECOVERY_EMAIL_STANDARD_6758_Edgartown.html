Our 4-Step Recovery Strategy

4. Adjust Price

Note: Adjusting price does not mean a lower sales price, it means a larger buyer pool. Lower prices usually produce higher prices (see below).

If some buyers call — Fall Back Price, $1,000,000

If we receive interest but no acceptable offer, we return to the market at $1,000,000 to get exposure to a larger buyer pool.

If no one calls — Pull the Rip Cord, $950,000

This suggests our price is not close to the buyer pool. We go to $950,000 to guarantee the largest buyer pool. Whatever buyers bid is what the home is worth at this time.

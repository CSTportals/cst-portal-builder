Our 4-Step Recovery Strategy

1. Inspect Property and Review Comments

Does the property need any updates that can make it more appealing to buyers?

2. Temporarily Off Market

Take the home TEMP Off Market. Buyers and agents watching the listing may call to find out why.

3. Test Buyer Interest

If they call, we explain that the home will return at a lower price. This may encourage buyers to make an offer before the reduction.

4. Adjust Price

Note: Adjusting price does not mean a lower sales price, it means a larger buyer pool. Lower prices usually produce higher prices (see below).

If some buyers call — Fall Back Price, $575,000

If we receive interest but no acceptable offer, we return to the market at $575,000 to get exposure to a larger buyer pool.

If no one calls — Pull the Rip Cord, $550,000

This suggests our price is not close to the buyer pool. We go to $550,000 to guarantee the largest buyer pool. Whatever buyers bid is what the home is worth at this time.
